The Story Behind Strong Filter

Strong Filter is developed for customers who prefer a stronger traditional filter-coffee profile. Arabica and Robusta are blended with 30% chicory. The ingredient proportions are presented openly to make choosing between the four SAARTHA ESTATE blends easier.

SAARTHA ESTATE is a coffee-growing estate in Mullur, Sanivarsanthe, Somwarpet Taluk, Kodagu (Coorg), Karnataka. Our website documents the estate, its crops and the gradual development of our coffee collection.
